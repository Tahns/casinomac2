## 4. Recommendation

Switch. The mining package is crowded and the lead competitors (Miner Cat, Prospector Capybara, Mining Rush, Dynamite Miner) are closer to us than the engine's distinctness rule allows. "Old Gus" collides with two Gus-named gold slots, which is a clear risk.

Ranking:
1. **Spy-jazz (Agent Mambo).** Lowest saturation (14), a strong adult mascot, and the fuse becomes a bomb-timer wire with no mapping stretch. The title is CLEAR. Risk: avoid anything that reads as James Bond, Pink Panther or Austin Powers.
2. **Treasure zeppelin (Captain Cloudburst).** No airship slots found, a lovable adult captain, and a natural line for the fuse (grapnel) and veins (cloud currents). The 1960s cartoon look fits well. Risk: the world needs more symbol variety than the other options.
3. **Ice-cream truck heist (Scoop Heist).** Playful, low saturation, and a distinctive palette. Risk: a heist plus ice cream may feel less premium, and the fuse mapping (melting drip) is weaker.

If the owner insists on keeping the mine: rename the mascot (drop "Gus"), drop "prospector plus TNT" in favor of one hook the rivals lack, and change the art away from the generic cartoon miner. This would still leave it in the most crowded cluster.

## 5. Effort to switch (math unchanged)

- Art: full redo. 10 symbols, background, mascot (poses and win states), logo and title treatment, UI skin, fuse and vein visuals, UNLEASH banner. This is the largest cost, estimated 3 to 6 weeks of artist time.
- Texts: rename the game, features, paytable and rules text, translations. About 2 to 3 days.
- Symbol set and mascot design: new character sheet, 1 to 2 weeks for design approval.
- Audio: new music bed, win stingers, fuse and UNLEASH cues. 1 to 2 weeks.
- Store and lobby art: tile, logo, thumbnails, promo clips. 3 to 5 days.
- Clearance: proper trademark search on the final title, 1 to 2 days of counsel time.
- Math and mechanics: untouched, only identifier and string renames in config.
